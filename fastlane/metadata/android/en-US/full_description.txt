Diktafon Pro is an independent fork of the open-source Diktafon voice recorder. It keeps the cassette-tape workflow and adds local audio import, editable note names, optional Gemini transcription and summaries, and ChatGPT OAuth summaries.

Your memos live on cassettes — one tape per topic, like "shopping", "kitchen renovation" or "book ideas". Each cassette plays as one continuous tape of your recordings, in order, just like the real thing.

<b>SIMPLE TO USE</b>
Controls you already know, inspired by real tape recorders. Open a cassette, press record, talk — no naming, no forms, no network needed. Rewind to find it again.

<b>MODERN</b>
Automatic transcriptions and summaries: every memo is transcribed, every cassette summarised and titled. Tap any word in a transcript and the tape jumps right there.

<b>CHOOSE HOW TO PROCESS YOUR AUDIO</b>
Run Whisper transcription and Qwen or Gemma summaries on your device. Or choose Gemini with your own API key: Gemini receives audio for transcription and transcript text for summaries. ChatGPT OAuth is available for summaries only and receives transcript text, never the audio. Cloud providers do not activate unless selected, and failures do not silently switch providers. Google may use content sent through its Free Tier to improve its products; paid-tier terms differ. Check current Google terms and pricing before sending private material.

<b>SPEAKS YOUR LANGUAGE</b>
Twenty languages: Arabic, Chinese (Simplified and Traditional), Czech, English, French, German, Hindi, Indonesian, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Persian, Polish, Portuguese, Russian, Spanish, Turkish, Ukrainian and Vietnamese — in recordings, summaries and the app itself. The spoken language is detected for each memo, so one tape can freely mix languages.

<b>FREE &amp; OPEN SOURCE</b>
No ads or lock-in. This independent fork is MIT-licensed: https://github.com/martitc/diktafon-pro

<b>AND THE LITTLE THINGS</b>
• Gapless playback with a colour-coded tape bar and an optional soft chime between memos.
• Export any cassette — or all of them — as a single archive with the audio and readable text inside; import only adds, never overwrites.
• Light &amp; dark themes, cassette colours, per-memo copy and delete.

A note on quality: available local models depend on your device's memory and storage. Cloud use requires your own Gemini API key or an authorized ChatGPT subscription.

Diktafon works best on 64-bit phones with 4 GB of RAM or more.
